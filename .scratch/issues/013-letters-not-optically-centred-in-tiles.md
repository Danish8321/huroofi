# Single letters sit off-centre in their tiles

Status: ready-for-agent · Type: bug · Found: letter-placement check 2026-10-04 on `main` 7630a60 (phone emulator)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Found
Single Arabic letters are centred by the font's line box, not by the glyph's visible ink. Noto Naskh puts each letter at a different height in that box, so placement drifts letter by letter. Measured on `huroofi_phone` (1080x2400) as ink centre minus tile centre:

- Look & listen letter tile (toddler, 118x136 dp): ب +34 dp low, its dot hangs out below the tile. أ is fine (−4 dp).
- Letter Map chips (40 dp): ر +7, س +8, ص +8, ع +7, م +9, ن +8, و +7, ي +9 dp low. ط ظ ف ك are within 1 dp. م and ي touch the bottom edge.
- Home stage strip chips (40 dp): same chip as the Map, same offsets.
- Parent zone letter grid (43x42 dp): same pattern, milder (م +6, ي +7, س ص ن about +5).
- Quiz prompt chip (64 dp): ذ −5.5 dp, within tolerance.
- Fine already: Lesson big letter and shape cells, Find words, and Trace and Paint guides (already ink-centred by `fitGlyph`, issue 003).
- Not checked: Reward screen (needs a stage finish).

## Decision
Centre each letter's ink in its own tile (maintainer, 2026-10-04). Baselines may differ between neighbouring chips. Do not keep a shared baseline.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Every single-letter tile centres the glyph's drawn ink, not its line box, so no letter sits low or spills out.

**Current behavior:**
- Tiles that show one Arabic letter (the stage chip used by Home and the Map, the zone letter grid, the Look & listen letter tile, `LetterChip`, the quiz prompt chip) lay the letter out with `ArabicText` / `Text` centred by line box.
- Letters with low bodies or tails (ر س ص ع م ن و ي) sit up to 9 dp low in a 40 dp chip.
- ب's dot leaves the Look & listen tile.

**Desired behavior:**
- One shared way to draw a single letter optically centred in a given box: ink bounding box centred both ways, fitting inside the box with a margin. Every single-letter tile uses it.
- Measuring a glyph's ink is cached per letter, size and density. It must not redraw offscreen on every frame.
- Font family (Noto Naskh Bold), colour and nominal size per tile stay as they are today. A letter is shrunk only when its ink would not otherwise fit.
- Accessibility semantics (content descriptions, text for TalkBack) are unchanged.
- Multi-letter words and inline Arabic in English sentences are not affected.

**Key interfaces:**
- `fitGlyph(measurer, letter, size, share, density)` already finds ink bounds by drawing offscreen. Reuse or extract its ink measurement rather than writing a second one.
- `StageLetterChip`, `LetterChip`, the zone grid cell, and the Look & listen letter tile should render through the new shared composable.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] On a 1080x2400 emulator, for every one of the 28 letters in Map and Home chips and the zone grid, the ink centre is within 10% of the tile size of the tile centre, both ways.
- [ ] No letter's ink touches or crosses its tile edge, including ب, ج, ي and ع on Look & listen.
- [ ] A unit or screenshot-free test covers the pure placement maths: given ink bounds and a box, it returns the offset (and scale, if any).
- [ ] `check.sh`, `test-fast.sh` and `contract.sh` pass.
- [ ] Before and after screenshots of Map, Home, the zone grid and Look & listen (ب) are attached in the commit or PR.

**Out of scope:**
- Arabic words (picture words, names, speech bubbles) and inline Arabic in English text.
- Trace and Paint guides (already ink-fitted).
- Changing letter sizes, colours, tile sizes or fonts beyond the shrink-to-fit above.
